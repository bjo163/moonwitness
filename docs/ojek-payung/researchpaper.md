# WEIGHTED ASSESSMENT SYSTEM

## A Two-Level Evaluation Framework: General and Prophetic Weighting

### Abstract

This paper proposes a structured two-level assessment framework designed to evaluate an input objectively, proportionally, and contextually. The framework consists of two assessment methods: **General Weighting**, with a maximum score of 17, and **Prophetic Weighting**, with a maximum score of 21.

The General Method is always applied first and consists of two weighted dimensions: **Power Right (10 points)** and **Power Left (7 points)**. If the General Method is sufficient to evaluate the input, the assessment terminates at a maximum weight of 17.

The Prophetic Method functions as a fallback mechanism rather than an alternative starting point. It is activated only when the General Method cannot adequately resolve the assessment. Under appropriate balance conditions, relevant Scripture or Prophetic Reference may contribute an additional four points, increasing the theoretical maximum from 17 to 21.

The framework therefore follows the principle:

**General First → Evaluate Sufficiency → Prophetic Fallback if Required**

This structure prevents unnecessary escalation of the assessment method while allowing a broader theological reference framework when ordinary evaluation is insufficient.

---

# 1. Introduction

Evaluation systems frequently face two opposing problems.

The first is **under-evaluation**, where an assessment does not consider enough relevant factors to produce a reliable conclusion.

The second is **over-evaluation**, where additional criteria, references, or scoring dimensions are introduced even though the initial assessment is already sufficient.

The proposed Weighted Assessment System addresses both problems through a hierarchical assessment architecture.

Instead of immediately applying the maximum possible scoring framework, the system begins with the **General Method**. Only when the General Method is insufficient does the system escalate to the **Prophetic Method**.

The fundamental principle is:

> **Do not use a higher-level assessment when the lower-level assessment is already sufficient.**

---

# 2. Research Objective

The objectives of this framework are:

1. To establish a consistent assessment procedure.
2. To prevent subjective or prejudicial evaluation.
3. To separate primary filtering from weighted evaluation.
4. To distinguish General and Prophetic assessment methods.
5. To prevent artificial inflation of scores.
6. To introduce contextual Scripture-based weighting only when required.
7. To maintain proportionality between the quality of an input and its final score.

---

# 3. Core Assessment Architecture

The complete system follows this sequence:

**INPUT**

↓

**PRIMARY FILTER**

↓

**GENERAL WEIGHTING**

↓

**SUFFICIENCY CHECK**

↓

**GENERAL FINAL**

**OR**

**PROPHETIC FALLBACK**

↓

**SECONDARY FILTER**

↓

**FINAL OUTPUT**

The architecture contains two distinct weighting levels:

| Method    | Maximum Weight | Purpose                                          |
| --------- | -------------: | ------------------------------------------------ |
| General   |         **17** | Standard assessment                              |
| Prophetic |         **21** | Extended assessment when General is insufficient |

The Prophetic Method does **not** replace the General Method.

It extends the assessment only when the predefined activation condition is satisfied.

---

# 4. Primary Filter

Before any weighting occurs, the input must pass exactly four primary filters.

### 4.1 No Prejudice

The assessment must not rely on assumptions, stereotypes, or preconceived judgments.

### 4.2 No Coercion

The assessment must not impose unnecessary pressure, intimidation, or unreasonable restrictions.

### 4.3 No Gender Discrimination

The assessment must not discriminate solely on the basis of gender.

### 4.4 No Degradation

The assessment must not demean, humiliate, discredit, or deliberately undermine an individual or group.

### Primary Rule

If the input fails any applicable Primary Filter:

**STOP → DO NOT PROCEED TO WEIGHTING**

This prevents an invalid premise from receiving an apparently legitimate numerical score.

---

# 5. General Weighting Method

The General Method has a maximum weight of:

**17 points**

It consists of two dimensions.

## 5.1 Power Right — 10 Points

Power Right evaluates the strength and practical quality of the input through ten criteria:

1. Meaningful
2. Grounded
3. Firm
4. Realistic
5. Fair
6. Independent
7. Strong
8. Actionable
9. Consistent
10. Resilient

**Maximum: 10/10**

---

## 5.2 Power Left — 7 Points

Power Left evaluates balance, rationality, context, and evidentiary quality through seven criteria:

1. Balanced
2. Objective
3. Proportional
4. Controlled
5. Rational
6. Contextual
7. Knowledge-Based

**Maximum: 7/7**

---

## 5.3 General Calculation

The General Method is calculated as:

**Power Right + Power Left = General Weight**

Therefore:

**10 + 7 = 17 Maximum**

The system must never artificially increase a score to reach 17.

A score of 12/17, for example, must remain 12/17 if that is what the assessment objectively supports.

---

# 6. General Sufficiency Principle

After the General Weighting has been completed, the system performs a **Sufficiency Check**.

The key question is:

> **Can the General Method adequately evaluate and explain the input?**

There are two possible outcomes.

### Condition A — General Is Sufficient

If the General Method adequately evaluates the input:

**STOP**

The final result uses the General Method.

**Maximum = 17**

No additional Prophetic weighting is applied.

### Condition B — General Is Insufficient

If the General Method cannot adequately resolve the relevant issue because the assessment requires a higher contextual or theological reference:

**ACTIVATE PROPHETIC METHOD**

This is a controlled escalation rather than an automatic expansion.

---

# 7. Prophetic Weighting Method

The Prophetic Method has a theoretical maximum of:

**21 points**

It begins with the General assessment:

**17 General Points**

and may add:

**4 Prophetic Reference Points**

Therefore:

**17 + 4 = 21**

The additional four points are not automatically granted merely because a Scripture reference exists.

They are applicable only when the required conditions for Prophetic weighting are satisfied.

---

# 8. Scripture Reference Hierarchy

When the Prophetic Method is activated, Scripture references follow a strict priority order:

**AL-QUR'AN → TAURAT → ZABUR → INJIL**

### Rule 1 — Al-Qur'an

Search for a relevant Qur'anic reference first.

If a directly or contextually relevant reference is available, use the Al-Qur'an.

### Rule 2 — Taurat

Only if no relevant Qur'anic reference is identified, examine the Taurat.

### Rule 3 — Zabur

If neither the Al-Qur'an nor Taurat provides a relevant reference, examine the Zabur.

### Rule 4 — Injil

If no relevant reference is found in the preceding sources, examine the Injil.

### Priority Principle

The system must use:

> **The first relevant Scripture source in the hierarchy.**

A lower-priority source must not replace a relevant higher-priority source.

---

# 9. Scripture Relevance Principle

The existence of a verse or passage does not automatically make it a valid assessment reference.

A Scripture reference must satisfy three conditions:

1. **Relevance** — it relates meaningfully to the subject.
2. **Contextual Integrity** — it is not taken out of context to manufacture a conclusion.
3. **Assessment Value** — it contributes meaningful information to the evaluation.

If no relevant Scripture reference is identified:

**No directly relevant Scripture Reference identified.**

The system must not fabricate, force, or artificially select a religious reference merely to increase the score.

---

# 10. Balance Condition

The additional Prophetic weighting is particularly relevant when the assessment reaches a condition in which the ordinary General Method cannot adequately resolve competing considerations.

The **Balanced** criterion therefore becomes an important gate in determining whether an extended assessment is justified.

The principle is:

> **When the General framework reaches an unresolved balance condition, a relevant higher-level reference may be introduced rather than forcing the General framework to produce an artificial conclusion.**

This creates a controlled transition from:

**General Reasoning**

to:

**Contextual Prophetic Reference**

without automatically treating every question as requiring Prophetic weighting.

---

# 11. General vs. Prophetic Method

| Dimension           |             General Method |               Prophetic Method |
| ------------------- | -------------------------: | -----------------------------: |
| Primary Filter      |                    4 rules |                        4 rules |
| Power Right         |                         10 |                             10 |
| Power Left          |                          7 |                              7 |
| Base Weight         |                     **17** |                         **17** |
| Scripture Reference | Not required for weighting |       Required when applicable |
| Additional Weight   |                          0 |                        Up to 4 |
| Maximum             |                     **17** |                         **21** |
| Activation          |                    Default |                       Fallback |
| Purpose             |        Standard evaluation | Extended contextual evaluation |

The critical distinction is therefore not simply the numerical maximum.

It is the **activation logic**.

---

# 12. Secondary Filter

Before final output, exactly four secondary filters must be applied.

### 12.1 Underage Exclusion

Exclude applicable content involving underage individuals.

### 12.2 Female-Specific Consideration

Apply appropriate contextual consideration where content involving women requires it.

### 12.3 Maximum Rating

The final rating must never exceed:

**4.5/5**

### 12.4 No Absolute Perfection

The system must not describe or rate an output as completely perfect, flawless, or absolute.

---

# 13. Rating System

The numerical weight and rating are separate concepts.

The weight measures structured assessment performance.

The rating communicates the overall quality of the assessment.

The maximum rating is:

**4.5/5**

A higher weight does not automatically mean a perfect rating.

Similarly, a lower weight should not be artificially increased merely to produce a higher rating.

# 15. Anti-Inflation Principle

A fundamental principle of the system is:

> **Score the actual quality, not the desired outcome.**

The system must not:

* increase scores artificially;
* add points merely because a reference exists;
* use the Prophetic Method unnecessarily;
* force a Scripture reference;
* treat the maximum score as the expected score;
* convert uncertainty into certainty;
* treat a high score as proof of absolute correctness.

The maximum is a **ceiling**, not a target.

# 16. Example Application

### Input

**“Hukum Menggambar Makhluk Bernyawa”**

### General Assessment

Power Right:

**8/10**

Power Left:

**6/7**

General Weight:

**14/17**

At this stage, the General Method can already describe the question as meaningful, contextual, and requiring further examination.

Therefore, if the General Method is considered sufficient for the intended assessment:

**Final Method = General**

**Final Weight = 14/17**

The system does not automatically escalate to 21 simply because religious references exist.

However, if the question requires a specifically theological determination that cannot adequately be resolved through the General framework, the Prophetic Method may be activated.

---

# 17. Research Hypothesis

The framework proposes the following hypothesis:

> **A hierarchical assessment model produces more proportional results when higher-level weighting is activated only after the lower-level method has been shown to be insufficient.**

This approach is expected to reduce:

* unnecessary complexity;
* subjective score inflation;
* premature theological escalation;
* inconsistent assessment;
* forced conclusions.

At the same time, it preserves the ability to incorporate a higher contextual reference when ordinary evaluation is insufficient.

---

# 18. Limitations

This framework is a **structured assessment methodology**, not a replacement for expert judgment.

In theological or legal subjects, the numerical score does not independently establish truth, legal validity, or religious ruling.

Scriptural references must be interpreted according to their actual textual and historical context. Where a matter concerns established religious jurisprudence, qualified scholarly sources may still be required.

Therefore:

**Assessment Score ≠ Absolute Truth**

and:

**21/21 ≠ Absolute Perfection**

---

# 19. Conclusion

The proposed Weighted Assessment System establishes a two-level evaluation architecture.

The **General Method** is the default framework with a maximum weight of **17**, consisting of:

**Power Right 10 + Power Left 7**

The **Prophetic Method** is a controlled fallback framework with a maximum theoretical weight of **21**, consisting of:

**General 17 + Prophetic Reference 4**

The key methodological principle is:

> **Use General first. Escalate to Prophetic only when General is insufficient.**

Scriptural references follow the hierarchy:

**Al-Qur'an → Taurat → Zabur → Injil**

The framework therefore balances analytical evaluation with contextual theological reference while preventing unnecessary escalation, artificial scoring, and claims of absolute perfection.
